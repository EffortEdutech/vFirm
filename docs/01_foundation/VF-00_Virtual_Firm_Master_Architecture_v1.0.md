## Core equation

**Professional Expertise + Virtual Workforce + Shared Business Infrastructure = Virtual Firm**

## Role boundaries

- **Client** buys from the Virtual Firm.
- **Professional Principal** owns the professional practice/brand and remains the human authority.
- **AI Workforce** performs governed routine, production, research, administration and bounded execution.
- **Platform** supplies professional practice infrastructure.
- **Network Specialists** add on-demand human or AI capability.
- **Regulated approval/sign-off** remains with an authorized human professional.
