## Purpose

Make the Virtual Firm feel like a professional 24/7 firm across website, portal, chat, email, WhatsApp and voice while keeping the professional brand front-facing.

## Core principles

1. Client buys from the Virtual Firm, not from AI.
2. Single front door with consistent context.
3. AI status answers must come from authoritative system records.
4. AI must not invent technical, commercial or legal facts.
5. Professional brand/white-label experience is first-class.
6. Lead capture and missing-information handling must be structured.
