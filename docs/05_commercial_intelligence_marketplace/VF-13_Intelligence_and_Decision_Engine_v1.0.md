## Purpose

Give one professional management-team visibility through multi-domain intelligence, scenario analysis, recommendations and decision memory.

## Core principles

1. Do not build a single unconstrained 'CEO Agent'.
2. Executive intelligence synthesizes multiple domains.
3. Professional attention is a scarce resource and should be optimized.
4. Recommendations must show evidence, options and consequences.
5. Decision memory should persist why decisions were made.
6. The Virtual Principal is an executive intelligence service, not a licensed professional.
