# VF-12 â€” Virtual Firm Commercial & Economic Engine

## Purpose

Ensure that the professional and platform can both operate sustainably by productizing services, measuring true delivery cost, pricing work, collecting cash and tracking profitability.

## Core principles

1. Separate client revenue, platform revenue and specialist payouts.
2. Service pricing must include professional, AI, specialist, software, compute, admin, insurance, payment and contingency costs.
3. Project and service contribution margin must be visible.
4. Commercial authority and discount limits are explicit.
5. Avoid commission-only platform economics.
6. Professional take-home economics are a north-star metric.
