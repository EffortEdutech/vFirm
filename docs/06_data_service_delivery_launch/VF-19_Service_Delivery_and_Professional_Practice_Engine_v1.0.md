### Assumption Register
Changes trigger impact analysis and possible variation.

### Dependency Register
Tracks required client/third-party inputs.

### Work Breakdown Structure
Every work package gets owner, workers, inputs, outputs, dependency, deadline, budget, risk and QA.

### Deliverable
A controlled professional artifact containing version, sources, calculations/references, QA, approval and delivery record.

## Change control

- included revision
