# SF-S6 Pilot Operator Handbook

## 1. Pilot boundary

This handbook is for controlled local operation of the first solopreneur Formwork Engineering Virtual Firm.

The pilot operator must keep these boundaries visible:

- the client buys from the Virtual Firm, not from AI;
- the Virtual Principal remains the human professional authority;
- workers assist, draft, check, and prepare only;
- no worker may approve scope, price, issue, certification, engineering conclusions, or payment instructions;
- live payment capture and public marketplace exposure remain outside this sprint.

## 2. Daily routine

Start each working day in Ops > Today.

Review in this order:

1. Exceptions.
2. Pending approvals and reviews.
3. Open deadlines and due-soon items.
4. Front Desk enquiry queue.
5. Project/task workload.
6. Technical delivery readiness.
7. Cash outstanding and approved expenses.
8. Recent activity and audit trail.

## 3. Representative working week

The local rehearsal is successful when the operator can demonstrate:

- enquiry capture, consent/conflict qualification, draft acknowledgement, and intake handoff;
- document/correspondence/deadline control;
- proposal preparation, principal approval, and accepted project opening;
- drawing revision comparison and deterministic input validation;
- technical delivery package ready for principal review only;
- expense and invoice preparation without autonomous payment action;
- daily operations summary with no HIGH/CRITICAL exceptions;
- human pilot handoff acceptance recorded with evidence.

## 4. Handoff acceptance

Record handoff only after the representative working-week rehearsal has passed.

The acceptance record should reference the rehearsal evidence and say whether the pilot is accepted, conditional, or blocked. Any issue affecting tenant isolation, professional authority, auditability, data protection, or commercial live-capture safety must be treated as a blocker before inviting external pilot users.

## 5. Stop rule

Do not define another sprint until SF-S6 acceptance is recorded or the product owner makes a new explicit scope decision.
