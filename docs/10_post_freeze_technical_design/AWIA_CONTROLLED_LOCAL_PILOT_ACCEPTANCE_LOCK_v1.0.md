## Acceptance Lock

The product owner authorizes AWIA virtual staff readiness for controlled local/private pilot operation inside the Virtual Firm Platform.

Accepted scope:

- named AWIA virtual staff model;
- package registry mapping across local Agent Skills;
- pilot staff provisioning;
- deterministic authority/runtime gate;
- AFCC staff management experience;
- evidence pack and controlled rehearsal;
- persisted staff records;
- staff lifecycle control;
- staff workdesk assignment;
- draft-only staff output;
- human output review; and
- client delivery draft preparation without final issue.

## Acceptance Statement

Bismillah... AWIA virtual staff controlled local pilot readiness is accepted with the locked boundaries below.

The platform may be demonstrated and rehearsed as a controlled local/private pilot where a client experiences hiring a Virtual Firm that operates named virtual staff under human authority.

## Locked Boundaries

This acceptance lock does not authorize:

- autonomous regulated approval;
- direct LLM to regulated final output;
- final client deliverable issue by virtual staff;
- live payment release;
- public marketplace operation;
- production launch;
- uncontrolled external data sharing;
- cross-tenant data mixing;
- claims that staff salary grants authority;
- claims that package binding grants authority; or
- claims that prompts/models/tools grant authority.
