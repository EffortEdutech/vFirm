- industry
- geography
- firm size
- service
- experience
- complexity
- client segment

Prefer percentile/peer comparison over naming competitors.

## AI productivity

Measure:
- AI completion
- human review time
- error/revision rate
- cost per deliverable
- time saved
- net quality-adjusted productivity

Do not equate raw speed with productivity.

## Service Performance Profile

For each service retain:
- demand
- complexity
- duration
- capacity
- price
- margin
- AI suitability
- human review
- risk
- repeatability

## Opportunity observatory

Detect emerging services and long-tail niches from:
- qualified demand
- unfulfilled requests
- searches
- repeated client questions
- project patterns
- regulatory change

## External intelligence

May combine platform data with lawful/licensed:
- public tenders
- government statistics
- industry reports
- regulatory updates
- procurement data
- economic indicators

- people

- clients
- projects
- revenue
- costs
- capacity
- risk
- pipeline

Scenarios are forecasts, not guaranteed outcomes.
