## Mission

Create an intelligent market for professional capability.

VF-23 answers:
1. What capability is demanded?
2. Where?
3. How much capacity exists?
4. Who can provide it?
5. What is the market value?
6. Where are shortages?
7. How should the ecosystem respond?

## Fundamental unit

**Available verified professional capability**, not headcount.

## Demand sources

- paid projects
- accepted proposals
- qualified client requests
- enterprise procurement
- marketplace searches
- unfulfilled requests
- lawful external market intelligence

Demand signals should be weighted by strength.

- qualification

## PCE

**Professional Capacity Equivalent** is a service-specific normalized measure of verified deliverable capacity. It should not pretend AI workers are literally equivalent to human professionals.

## Market states

- Critical Shortage
- Shortage
- Tight
- Balanced
- Abundant
- Oversupplied

## Supply response

Possible responses:
- redistribute capacity
- activate idle professionals
- recruit professionals
- launch firms
- train/assess professionals
- add AI capacity
- add software/tools
- add specialists
- geographic expansion
- policy-controlled pricing signals

## Professional Capacity Exchange

Allow firms/professionals to expose time-bounded available capacity and allow enterprise capacity reservation/pools/surge capacity.

## Opportunity discovery

Identify:
- long-tail professional niches
- capacity shortages
- adjacent services
- geographic opportunities
- training/recruitment needs

Commercial demand and legal eligibility are separate.

## Follow-the-sun

Route work across time zones only where security, client agreement, professional regulation and handoff quality allow it.
